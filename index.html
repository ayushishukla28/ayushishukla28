01 — About

A people-first mind with an analytical streak.

I'm currently in the first year of my MBA at NMIMS, where I'm exploring two paths that I believe belong together: Human Resources and Data Analytics.

Organisations run on people, and the best people decisions are increasingly made with evidence — from hiring and engagement to retention and growth. I want to be the person who understands both sides: the human context and the data behind it.

Outside the classroom, I'm building hands-on skills through projects, case competitions and coursework. This site is where I'll share that journey.
